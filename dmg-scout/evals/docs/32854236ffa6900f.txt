• Capital Projects Manager Chris Trotta reported that routing easements designs and
pipeline design are completed, and easements are being secured for the VC water line
replacement from I-580 to the top of the siphon.
• For the Courthouse Fence project, Lodi Iron Works is on track to have the first casting
done and submitted for approval by the end of January. We are working with the
Comstock Foundation Blacksmith to recreate the clips needed to secure the bottoms of
the new slats. The project will install in the spring.
• For Piper’s Oprea House, Bidding is complete for the refurbishment of 12 entry doors
and 5 southside windows. We will next proceed with contracts and material submittals.
We now have an approved design and pricing for the seismic upgrade at the entry
column, and we will schedule the work as soon as the connectors are received.
• For Water Tank Upgrades, the program pricing for rehab and maintenance for all seven
domestic water tanks has been reviewed and has been referred to the Comptroller.
• For the Gold Hill Collection System Piping Replacement. Pre-design engineering and
environmental work is underway.

24.DISCUSSION/FOR POSSIBLE ACTION: Discussion and a general brief of the 2025
Special Session of the Nevada Legislature.
Will Adler of Silver State Government Relations said his organization was not that
passionately involved in the special session of the Legislature. Storey County didn’t have
issues directly involved in the proposed bills. Mr. Adler said the special session went from
Dec. 13-19, and included a crime bill, film tax credits and studio for Las Vegas, statewide
health care access, and issues with Winston Park in Las Vegas. There were also items on
cybersecurity.

like the team to coordinate with NACO and challenge how the bill was passed.
