Emisha Innovations proudly embeds equity, diversity, and inclusion (EDI) into its core operating principles. As a Black-owned technology company, Emisha is committed to

Inclusive Hiring: We actively recruit talent from underrepresented backgrounds in STEM, including through partnerships with HBCUs, HSIs, and community colleges.
