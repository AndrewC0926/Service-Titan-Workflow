On behalf of Taylor Print & Visual Impressions, Inc. (“TPVI”), located at 4151 N. Pecos Rd.,
Suite 203, Las Vegas, Nevada 89115, we respectfully submit this letter to accompany our
Nevada Standard Tax Abatement Incentive Application for the following abatements: Sales
& Use Tax, Modified Business Tax, and Personal Property Tax.
TPVI is a commercial printing manufacturer and solutions provider supporting customers
who require high-quality print production, secure data-driven communication, and fast
turnarounds. Our Las Vegas operations include offset & digital print and mail, short- and
medium-run folding carton packaging, and wide-format rigid and roll printing and
finishing. Our facility maintains recognized compliance and production standards,
including HIPAA HITRUST certification (enabling processing and handling of protected
health information for mailings) and industry certifications such as G7® Color
Management and seamless USPS mailings.
1) Significance of the abatement in the decision to expand in Nevada
The requested abatements are a significant factor in our decision to expand in Nevada
because they directly improve the economics of manufacturing investment in high-capital,
high-throughput printing and finishing equipment—investments that drive productivity,
quality, and competitiveness. In commercial print manufacturing, customer demands for
shorter runs, rapid turnaround, and consistent color require continual reinvestment in
presses, automation, finishing, and workflow technology. This is reflected in our $3.5
million purchase and installation of a new 8-color Offset press in our Las Vegas facility.
Although that investment was made before we could apply for and benefit from the
abatement, we are still committed to continuing to invest in manufacturing equipment and
staffing in Las Vegas as reflected in the attached application. The Nevada abatements will
help offset the up-front tax cost associated with expansion-related equipment purchases
2
and facility improvements, allowing us to allocate more capital toward production
modernization, workforce training, and sustained growth in Nevada rather than shifting
investment to other regions competing for this work.
In practical terms, the abatements help us accelerate the scale and timing of our Nevada
expansion by supporting near-term capital and operational expenditures that are essential
for manufacturing ramp-up—particularly as we expand capacity to serve hospitality,
healthcare communications, packaging, and election-related production needs in Nevada
as well as the western U.S.
2) Major markets for products manufactured or distributed from the Nevada facility
Our Nevada facility is strategically positioned to serve both local and regional markets. The
Las Vegas operation is also a preferred vendor partner to leading hospitality brands in
the Las Vegas area. Beyond the local hospitality market, we support customers across the
Western and Southwestern United States, including segments such as healthcare
communications, consumer packaging, and elections services.
Because commercial printing frequently involves time-sensitive delivery windows (events,
campaigns, elections, and healthcare communications), the Las Vegas location enables
faster production and distribution to customers throughout the region while supporting
service-level expectations for quality, speed, and reliability.
3) Job growth plans and whether hiring will be from within Nevada
Our expansion plan includes meaningful job growth in Nevada to support increased
production volume, expanded capabilities, and enhanced customer service. Based on
internal planning for our Las Vegas operations, we anticipate increasing headcount from
fewer than 54 employees at the start of 2026 to greater than 75 employees as growth
initiatives progress—representing approximately 25-35 new jobs over the next couple of
years.
We intend for the majority of hiring to be sourced within Nevada, leveraging the local
workforce for manufacturing, production, logistics, and administrative roles. In a
commercial printing operation, these positions commonly include press and digital
equipment operators, bindery/finishing technicians, wide-format production staff,
prepress/workflow specialists, mail and fulfillment teams, quality/color management
roles, and shipping/logistics support. As we hire, we also prioritize internal development
through training and upskilling so that employees can advance into higher-skill
manufacturing roles as new equipment and processes come online.

Company history & Nevada growth plan. TPVI is part of the Taylor family of businesses

customer partnership. In Nevada, our plan is to expand manufacturing capacity and
distribution capabilities to meet growing regional demand across hospitality, healthcare
communications, consumer packaging, and election-related services.
A key element of our operational plan is continued investment in modern manufacturing
technology and production efficiency. This includes scaling core capabilities including
offset and digital print and mail, folding carton packaging, and wide-format production
to support both short-run agility and consistent, repeatable output at commercial volumes.
As part of our broader expansion activity, our organization has also undertaken initiatives to

of a new press, and will continue to make major equipment investments that support
higher throughput and advanced print manufacturing output.
Community involvement. TPVI is committed to being a stable, long-term employer and

workflows, and improving production yields—which supports both environmental
responsibility and manufacturing cost control.
