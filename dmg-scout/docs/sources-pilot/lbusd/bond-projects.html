Bond Funds At Work

Welcome to our District Bond Project page, where you can find information on the exciting Measure E, K and Q renovation and construction activities happening across Long Beach Unified School District. We’re committed to enhancing our schools with state-of-the-art facilities that support student learning and community growth. Check back regularly for the latest updates and feel free to reach out if you have any questions about our ongoing projects.
